# Participant Informed Consent — Limited Field Validation

## English

**Study**: Usability validation of the SapiSehat cattle disease early-detection app
**Conducted by**: SapiSehat project team, Politeknik Negeri Semarang

### What this is
You are invited to help test a mobile app that gives an **early indication** of
cattle disease (Foot and Mouth Disease, Lumpy Skin Disease, or healthy) from a
photo. We are testing the **app's usability**, not your ability and not the
medical accuracy of the app.

### Important
- This app provides an **early indication only**. It is **NOT a diagnosis** and
  **NOT a replacement for a veterinarian**.
- This session is **not** a medical or clinical study.

### What you will do
- Answer a few background questions (your role, experience, phone familiarity).
- Try several tasks in the app while thinking aloud.
- Answer questions about what the results mean.
- Give your opinion.
This takes about 45 minutes.

### Your data & privacy
- We record only **non-identifying** information (your role and what happened
  on screen). We do **not** record your face or full name in results.
- Cattle photos and any location stay on the device per the app's privacy model.
- Your responses are anonymized in any report.

### Voluntary participation
Participation is voluntary. You may **stop at any time** without giving a reason.

Anda diundang untuk membantu menguji aplikasi yang memberikan **indikasi awal**
penyakit sapi (PMK, Lato-Lato/LSD, atau sehat) dari sebuah foto. Kami menguji

### Penting
- Aplikasi ini hanya memberikan **indikasi awal**. Ini **BUKAN diagnosis** dan
  **BUKAN pengganti dokter hewan**.
