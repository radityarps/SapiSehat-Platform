# SapiSehat Product

## Register

SapiSehat Platform

## Users

- Farmer users use mobile app.
- Agency users use web dashboard.
- No farmer web dashboard in first release.

## Product Purpose

SapiSehat gives early cattle disease-risk indication and follow-up prioritization.

It helps farmers record cattle, scan images, and review detection history.

It helps agency users review jurisdiction-scoped risk items and record follow-up status.

It does not provide veterinary diagnosis or confirmed outbreak declaration.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable.

Mobile app should feel like field tool for daily use: clear, quiet, trustworthy, and easy on bad screens.

## Anti-references

- No clinical diagnosis app framing.
- No alarmist red emergency styling.
- No decorative SaaS noise.
- No cramped government portal clutter.
- No confusing status language.

## Design Principles

1. Safe wording stays near interpretation points.
2. Mobile screens stay task-first.
3. Main flow is login, cattle, scan, history.
4. Large touch targets matter.
5. Evidence state should be visible before certainty.
6. Offline flow must feel reliable, not broken.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA.

Design for Android users on mixed-quality phones, daylight glare, and low-tech field use.

Use readable contrast, visible focus, clear loading and empty states, and reduced-motion alternatives.

Do not rely on color alone for disease class, reliability, or sync state.
