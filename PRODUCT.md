# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agency officers in Indonesian livestock or animal-health agencies use the dashboard during office triage and follow-up coordination. They review jurisdiction-scoped cattle disease-risk submissions, prioritize review items, and record follow-up status without claiming diagnosis or outbreak confirmation.

Secondary users are ops/admin reviewers who inspect audit logs and backend activity when validating release behavior or investigating system actions.

## Product Purpose

SapiSehat turns farmer-submitted cattle records, image evidence, future NLP evidence, fusion results, cluster risk signals, follow-ups, and audit logs into safe agency work queues.

Success means agency users can quickly answer:

- which review items need attention,
- which district-level risk signals need follow-up priority,
- what evidence state exists for each item,
- what follow-up was recorded,
- which internal action occurred and when.

The product supports early disease-risk monitoring and follow-up prioritization. It does not provide veterinary diagnosis, infected-case proof, or outbreak declaration.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable.

The interface should feel like a reliable operational tool for public animal-health monitoring: clear enough for daily triage, restrained enough to avoid alarm, and explicit about evidence limits.

## Anti-references

Avoid dated government portal patterns: cramped pages, weak hierarchy, heavy form blocks, inconsistent controls, low-contrast gray text, and table-only pages with no decision support.

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard clichés when possible: oversized vanity metrics, decorative gradients, icon-card grids, and unclear status colors.

Avoid clinical diagnosis software framing: red emergency styling, patient-chart metaphors, disease-positive language, or any interface that implies confirmed diagnosis.

## Design Principles

1. Safe wording stays near interpretation points. Any risk label, signal, or review item should make scope clear.
2. Tables are work surfaces, not decoration. Sorting, filtering, density, and row actions matter more than metric cards.
3. Jurisdiction boundaries are visible. Users should understand that data is scoped, not global.
4. Evidence state comes before certainty. Image-only, NLP unavailable, reliability, and conflict status must be legible.
5. Follow-up is action, not spectacle. Primary actions should guide officers to record useful next steps with minimal visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA.

Design for agency users on mixed-quality office monitors and laptops, with possible daylight glare and long review sessions. Use readable contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-reachable navigation and table actions, clear loading and empty states, and reduced-motion alternatives for all motion.

Do not rely on color alone for disease class, risk level, follow-up status, or reliability state.
